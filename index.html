01 — About

Why science needs good governance

While studying genetics and artificial intelligence, I realized that the biggest breakthroughs in healthcare, agriculture, and climate resilience don't fail in the lab—they fail in last-mile delivery. Good governance is the operating system that brings science to the citizen.

Alongside my undergraduate engineering coursework, I am actively preparing for the Civil Services Examination, building a foundation in Indian polity, economics, and ethical leadership.

“Good governance is not merely administration; it is the ethical application of knowledge for the welfare of the last person in line.”
